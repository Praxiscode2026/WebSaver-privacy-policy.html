WebVid Saver — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 3 November 2026

Summary

WebVid Saver is a free, ad-supported app made by PraxisCode. We (the developer) do not run any server, we do not have user accounts, and we do not collect or store your personal information. The app saves media to your own device. To keep the app free, we show ads through Google AdMob, and the app requests non-personalized ads only. The app does not track you across other apps or websites and does not ask for App Tracking Transparency permission.

Information the developer collects

None. WebVid Saver has no accounts, no login, and no backend server. The videos and files you save stay on your device (and, if you choose, in your Photos library).

Advertising (Google AdMob)

To keep the app free, we display ads using Google AdMob. Because the app serves non-personalized ads and never requests the App Tracking Transparency prompt, it does not access your device's advertising identifier (IDFA) to track you. In the course of serving ads, Google AdMob may collect a limited amount of data, including:

  • a non-identifying device identifier used for ad delivery and frequency capping;
  • app usage and ad-interaction data (for example, that an ad was shown or tapped);
  • crash and performance diagnostics to keep the ads and app working;
  • approximate, IP-based location (general region only).

This data is used to serve non-personalized ads, prevent fraud, and measure ad performance. It is not used to track you across other companies' apps or websites, and it is not sold or shared with data brokers. You can read how Google uses data in the Google Privacy Policy and the AdMob data-use guide.

Photos access

If you choose to save a video to your Photos library, the app uses add-only Photos access. This lets the app add a video you selected; it cannot read or view your existing photos.

Websites you open

When you enter a link in the app, an in-app viewer opens that website so you can browse it. Those third-party websites are operated by others and have their own privacy policies; this policy does not cover them. Please save only content you have the right to keep.

Children

WebVid Saver is a general-audience app and is not directed to children under 13.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The "last updated" date above shows when it last changed.
