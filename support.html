WebVid Saver — Support

What the app does

WebVid Saver is a personal, offline media library for your iPhone and iPad. You add media that you are entitled to keep, then play, organize, and save it — all on your device.

Adding media

  • From a link: On the Library tab, tap + then Add from Link, enter a web address, and the page opens in an in-app viewer. Browse to the video, and when a download badge appears, tap it and choose the video to save.
  • From your files: Tap + then Import from Files to bring in video or audio from the Files app or iCloud Drive.

Your Library

Saved media appears on the Library tab, where you can play it, search, rename it, export it to your Photos library, or delete it.

Which sites are blocked

To respect their terms of service, saving is disabled on major social and streaming platforms (including YouTube, Instagram, TikTok, Facebook, X, Reddit, Vimeo, Twitch, and paid streaming services). You can still browse those sites, but the save option is turned off there.

Why a video won't save

Some websites stream video in protected or non-downloadable formats, or require a login, so the app may not be able to save them. Try a site that permits downloading, such as the Internet Archive or Wikimedia Commons.
